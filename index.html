See HealthyClaim in Action

Watch our real product demos showing how HealthyClaim works inside your existing EHR system — no setup needed.

Eligibility Verification: HealthyClaim verifies patient insurance eligibility in real-time directly inside your EHR — eliminating manual lookups and reducing front-desk errors before claims are even submitted.

Prior Authorization: Automatically identify procedures requiring prior auth, gather the right documentation, and submit to payers — saving hours of phone calls and administrative burden per case.

Claim Appeal: When denials happen, HealthyClaim drafts a targeted appeal letter using the denial reason code, payer-specific rules, and your clinical documentation — turning denials into recovered revenue.

Engineering Transparency

What HealthyClaim Is NOT

Clear boundaries are essential when applying AI to healthcare revenue cycle intelligence.

Strict Boundary

NOT an LLM that Invents CPT Codes

We never permit probabilistic language models to hallucinate or guess billing codes. Candidate codes must be deterministically grounded in extracted clinical facts and authoritative code sets.

Strict Boundary

NOT a Chatbot Making Billing Decisions

AI is restricted to bounded linguistic evidence extraction. All coding, unbundling, modifier, and unit determinations are computed deterministically by verified rules.

Strict Boundary

NOT a Production Clearinghouse

HealthyClaim generates draft claim artifacts (CMS-1500, EDI 837P) for evaluation, testing, and downstream clearinghouse intake — not live electronic claim transmission.

Strict Boundary

NOT a Substitute for Human Review

When clinical documentation is ambiguous, the system surfaces clarification requests rather than making silent guesses. Certified coders retain final oversight.

Multi-Layer Accuracy Architecture

5 Independent Layers of Clinical Verification

Every single claim runs through five sequential accuracy gates before an artifact is generated. No single point of failure. No unverified assumptions.

01
Layer 1 • Clinical Evidence Extraction

Linguistic Fact Parsing & Assertion Verification

De-identifies and extracts factual clinical observations (symptoms, diagnoses, procedural details, anatomical laterality, and encounter duration). Filters out historical or ruled-out conditions to prevent false claims.

Fact Verified
02
Layer 2 • Authoritative Coding Rules

CMS NCCI Edits & MUE Unit Caps

Evaluates Column 1 and Column 2 unbundling conflicts against official CMS NCCI tables. Determines modifier eligibility and enforces strict practitioner and facility MUE unit ceilings.

NCCI / MUE Evaluated
03
Layer 3 • Coverage & Medical Necessity

Medicare LCD, NCD & Coverage Cross-Referencing

Validates diagnostic medical necessity against official Medicare Local Coverage Determinations (LCD) and National Coverage Determinations (NCD) to identify non-covered indications.

LCD/NCD Matched
04
Layer 4 • Coding Rule Evaluation

Evidence-Grounded Rule Calculations

Computes the documented E/M level via 2-of-3 MDM matrix and time analysis; evaluates Modifier 50 for bilateral criteria; splits JW/JZ drug waste per CMS Ch.17; and applies the 8-minute therapy remainder rule.

Rules Evaluated
05
Layer 5 • Draft Artifact Validation

Draft ANSI EDI 837 & CMS-1500 Formatting

Performs 5010 structural validation, diagnosis pointer mapping (1–4 pointers per line), POS facility vs non-facility rate checks, and generates draft CMS-1500, UB-04, and ANSI 837 EDI files for testing.

Formatted Draft Artifact

One Deterministic Engine. Four Essential Workflows.

From pre-submission claim generation to post-denial appeals, HealthyClaim covers the full spectrum of revenue cycle operations.

Workflow 01

Claim Generation (Code & Bill)

Convert raw clinical notes into draft CMS-1500 and UB-04 claims in seconds. NCCI, MUE, and modifier rules are resolved deterministically before downstream processing.

  • E/M 2-of-3 MDM & Time Auto-Leveling
  • Modifier 50 Bilateral & Add-on Inclusion

Workflow 02

Prior Authorization

Map documented clinical evidence directly to payer-specific medical coverage criteria. Identify missing diagnostic tests or documentation gaps before submission.

Draft claim artifacts produced by HealthyClaim are evaluated against authoritative CMS NCCI, MUE, and Medicare coverage data for testing and research purposes.
